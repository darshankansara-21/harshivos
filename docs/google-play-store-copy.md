## Full description

WonderPlay is a calm, playful world made for every child — a single, gentle app
where they can explore, learn, communicate and settle down.

There are no ads, no sign-ups and nothing to buy. Everything stays on your
device. Just open it and play.

WonderPlay is built around five simple doors:

🧸 PLAY
Dozens of hands-on toys and gentle games — pop bubbles, smooth sand, swirl
fluid, guide a slither snake, sort colours, trace shapes, and much more. Every
toy responds instantly to touch with soft sound and motion.

🌊 CALM
Soothing sensory experiences and simple breathing to help a child slow down and
settle — ripple water, smooth kinetic sand, trace calm constellations in Star
Weaver, gather fireflies in Firefly Glow, conduct slow orbits in Gravity Garden,
or stack a quiet cairn in Zen Stones. Adjustable sound and reduced motion keep
it comfortable.

💬 TALK
A friendly picture board. Tap a card and WonderPlay speaks it out loud, so a
child can ask for what they need. Recently used words stay close at hand.

🎓 LEARN
Playful matching, emotions, counting and pattern activities that grow with the
child.

📅 ROUTINES
Gentle visual routines to make everyday moments feel calmer and more
predictable.

Meet Hari and Pico — a warm little friend and their playful puppy — who greet
your child and keep them company along the way.

MADE WITH CARE
• Works fully offline — no internet needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts
• Nothing leaves the device — play stays private
• Adjustable sound (off / soft / normal) and reduced-motion options
• Big, forgiving touch targets and a quiet, uncluttered look

WonderPlay is a playful companion for exploration, communication and calm — a
safe corner of the screen where a child can simply be themselves.
